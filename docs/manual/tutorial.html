How To Use This

Pick a tab, move one slider at a time, and watch how the plots and explanations change. The page is written for students who know the experiment but not yet the theory language.

Key Ideas

  • Smaller F means better precision.
  • F^-2 is information efficiency per collected photon.
  • Throughput matters because a great estimator is not enough if the instrument discards too many photons.
  • Gate position changes both photon count and information content.

One-line Intuition

F tells you how much worse the precision is than the ideal case. F^-2 tells you what fraction of your photons are effectively useful. R tells you how easily the lifetime can be separated from noise at the current photon count.
